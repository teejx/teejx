# Motion notes

- The README uses a vertical sequence: hero, contact links, roles, tech stack, and GitHub streak.
- Concert One is used across the hero, cards, and contact-button artwork.
- The hero keeps its coordinated text entrance and slow in-place ornament rotation.
- Contact pills and the roles and tech cards use restrained entrance glides. Tech labels and icons arrive with the card; its decorative star stays still.
- The extra shapes in the contact, roles, tech, and streak sections do not float or spin.
- Reduced-motion mode shows a static hero; the cards expose their still layout when motion is reduced.
- The live streak service uses its own SVG output. The local composite preview uses a placeholder for that data.

Motion principles adapted from [rossark-motion-skill](https://github.com/rossark0/rossark-motion-skill): calm timing, small amplitudes, continuous movement, and a good static frame.

The rounded petal ornament is adapted from “Flower Star Rectangle 8 Round” in the [SVGBackgrounds geometric shape library](https://www.svgbackgrounds.com/elements/svg-shapes-geometric/); see `README-SETUP.md` for asset notes.
